# El Toro: design notes

The map is based on the "El Toro 20", the 20-stair at El Toro High School in Lake Forest,
California. It was skated from 1999 until the school removed it in June 2019. Apart from
the place name, the map uses no school names, logos or branding. Everything is plain
concrete, per STYLE.md.

## What the research found

- **The set:** 20 concrete steps, about 13 ft (4 m) of drop over about 17 ft (5.2 m).
  It was a wide, steep set, known for "near perfect run up positioning" and "the
  unforgiving concrete that awaits at the bottom".
  ([ArchiveThreads](https://www.archivethreads.ca/post/visiting-el-toro-20-the-behemoth-stairset-breaking-necks-since-1999),
  [SkateSided](https://skatesided.com/el-toro-the-legendary-stair-set-of-southern-california/))
- **The handrails:** there were three: one down each side and one down the middle.
  Heath Kirchart's lipslide in *The End* (1998–99) was on the centre rail. That rail was
  removed around 2010, and later clips use the side rails (e.g. "the right-hand side
  rail").
  ([Wikipedia: El Toro High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Toro_High_School),
  [Artefact](https://www.artefactmagazine.com/2020/01/28/skateboardings-gnarliest-spot-el-toro/),
  [Taming El Toro (mpora archive)](https://www.active-traveller.com/mpora-archive/taming-el-toro))
- **The setting:** the set ran down a slope between two levels of the campus. When it
  was demolished, "an empty sandy slope" was left. "Above and off to the side of the
  stairs is one of the school's buildings." Dylan Stark later dropped off that roof onto
  the slope.
  ([Vital MTB](https://www.vitalmtb.com/videos/member/Behind-the-Scenes-Watch-Dylan-Stark-STOMP-the-El-Toro-Roof-Drop,36766/bturman,109),
  [Unofficial Networks](https://unofficialnetworks.com/2019/06/21/el-toro-mtb/))
- **The rebuild:** the set no longer met the stair safety code. Talk at the time was of
  turning it into a double set, with bollards at the top and bottom.
  ([Be-Mag](https://be-mag.com/article/column-the-legacy-of-el-toro/))
- **The campus:** the spot sits inside the school grounds, behind gates, and was usually
  skated at weekends. Riders also used a fence-to-rail transfer, and there was a
  "22 stair rail" by the tennis courts nearby.
  ([FindSkateSpots](https://findskatespots.com/spots/lake-forest-ca/ridge-route-drive-el-toro-20),
  [Be-Mag](https://be-mag.com/article/column-the-legacy-of-el-toro/))

The sources don't describe the exact layout at the top and bottom: the quad, the lunch
area, the planters, or the length of the run-out. Those parts are a typical Southern
California campus, built to fit the facts above.

## What the map reproduces, and what it simplifies

| Real spot | In the map |
|---|---|
| 20 steps, ≈ 4 m drop over ≈ 5.2 m | 20 × (0.165 rise, 0.30 run) = **3.3 m over 6 m**, 4 m wide. This is kept exactly as before, so the ollie, the kickflip and the handrail 50-50 still land. It is a bit less steep and less tall than the real set. |
| Three rails; the middle one gone after ≈ 2010 | **One handrail down each side** (the post-2010 set), 0.38 m above the nosings. The rails end in the air just past the foot. There is no centre rail. |
| Single set (a double set was only proposed) | A single set, as it was skated. |
| Upper campus with a run-up | **Upper quad**, 16 × 12 m, 3.33 m up. It is ONE funbox piece with walls all round, so the 14 m run-up has no seams (ADR 0008). The spawn is 14 m behind the lip, as before. |
| A building above and beside the stairs | **Classroom blocks**, one storey above the quad, on three sides: the back, the south wing (−Z) and the north wing (+Z). The south wing is beside the stairs, like the real one. |
| Covered walkways | A **covered walkway** along the south wing on the quad: 4 posts and a flat roof 2.7 m up. There is also a bench wall under it. |
| Lunch area | **Two lunch-table sets** on the quad's north side. Each set is a 0.72 m table between two 0.44 m benches, which you can grind or manual. |
| Slopes beside the set (the "sandy slope") | Low **planter beds** beside the foot of the set on both sides, and planters guarding the drop edge on the quad. These are simplified: the real slopes are left out. |
| Landing onto campus concrete | A **lower courtyard**. It has 17 m of clear roll-out past the foot, then the fence line. |
| Way back up | A **10° walkway ramp** (ADA-style, though steeper than a real 1:12 ramp) that runs along the quad's north retaining wall. It goes from the courtyard up to a landing at the back of the quad, with a 0.9 m guard rail against the north wing. Pushing up it works (scenario). The landing stands 3 cm above the quad and overlaps its edge, so you roll off a small lip and never hit a seam. |
| Other spots on campus | See "Secondary spots" below. |
| Gates and a school fence | A **fence line** of 0.9 m barriers round the courtyard (east, north and south), with the sponsor banners. |

The whole map fits in about 42 × 24 m (x −19…23.3, z −11.1…12.8). A test checks that
it stays within 43 × 30 m.

## Secondary spots

- **4-stair off the lower terrace** (x 13–19.5, z −10.4…−6.4). The terrace is a raised
  walk 0.69 m high, made as one funbox piece. It has 15° banks on its +X and +Z sides
  (a hip) to roll up from the courtyard. The 4-stair goes down toward −X, and its
  platform is buried 3 cm under the terrace, the same way the 20's is. It lands into
  a 12 m strip in front of the south wing. Scenario: an ollie down it.
- **Planter ledge** (x 8–14, z 9.6–11.2). A raised planter bed with a 0.38 m concrete
  wall. Scenario: a 50-50 on its near edge, then an ollie out.
- **Curb** (x 9–19, z 4–4.25, 0.15 m) between the landing walk and the courtyard.
- **Lunch benches** (0.44 m) and the **bench wall** under the walkway on the quad.

## Seams (ADR 0008)

- The quad is one convex piece. The 20-stair's platform lies 3 cm under it.
- The terrace is one convex piece. The 4-stair's platform lies 3 cm under it.
- The ramp's landing lies 3 cm above the quad and overlaps its north edge by 0.3 m.
- Every other block stands on a deck or on the ground, and no riding surface meets
  another flush. `el-toro.test.ts` checks all of this.

## Barriers, sponsor banners and graffiti

Built in `el-toro-dressing.ts` from `EL_TORO.fence`, `wallBanners` and `graffiti` (tested
in `el-toro.test.ts`).

- **Fence line:** the three low courtyard walls are now `barrier` segments, 0.9 m high
  and ≈ 3.9 m long (solid, never grindable), ids `fence-<side>-<n>`: east
  (x 23–23.3, z −10.8…12.5, behind the roll-out, square to the drop), north
  (z 12.5–12.8, x 0…23.3) and south (z −11.1…−10.8, x 0…23.3). The quad and the
  classroom blocks close the west. (The first plan had the south fence double as a
  fence-to-rail transfer; barriers are never grindable, so that one is gone.)
- **Banners:**
  1. **East fence:** BipBop Labs (z −3…0.85) and v4rgas (z 0.85…4.7) side by side,
     facing −X, the backdrop of the landing seen from the top of the stairs and in the
     montage tripod shot.
  2. **North fence:** the same pair behind the planter ledge (v4rgas x 7.8–11.7,
     BipBop Labs x 11.7–15.5), facing −Z.
  3. **Retaining walls beside the stairs:** a thin banner board (2.8 × 0.7 m, 8 cm
     thick) fixed to each x = 0 wall, 1.9–2.6 m up, facing the courtyard: BipBop Labs on
     the north wall (z 2.8…5.6), v4rgas on the south wall (z −5.6…−2.8). They stay
     outside the handrail approaches (|z| ≤ 2.7) and below the lip.
  The fence's other segments are plain concrete (no house banners); every sponsor pair
  has plain wall either side.
- **Graffiti** (two pieces, walls only, never the stairs or a riding surface):
  - a `v4rgas-throwup` on the south wing's end wall (x = 0, z ≈ −8.4), the backdrop of
    the 4-stair's landing;
  - a `penguin-king` on the quad's north retaining wall above the walkway ramp (z = 6,
    x ≈ −4, ≈ 2.5 m up, clear of the ramp's surface), seen pushing up the ramp.
  The retaining walls beside the stairs carry the banner boards instead of paint, and the
  terrace's west wall is taken up by the 4-stair itself.
